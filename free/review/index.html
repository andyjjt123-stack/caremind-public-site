Two dedicated demo cameras stream generated color frames and silent audio. They expose no household camera, microphone, recordings, or household pairing data.

The app interface is in Traditional Chinese. This English guide includes the exact on-screen labels.

1. Review family mode with the synthetic demos

You can complete this part with one Android phone and this page. The demo cameras are already running; you do not need to create a camera pairing or use a second physical camera.

  1. Install and open the submitted CareMind Free app (tw.caremind.basic). Choose This device is the family viewer (這台當家屬端). If the app reopens a saved role, use Settings and help (設定與說明) → Switch role (切換用途).
  2. Copy the complete camera A invitation below. In the app expand Cannot scan? Use a private pairing link (無法掃碼？使用私人配對連結); paste into 貼上私人配對連結; tap Check and add camera (檢查並加入鏡頭), then Confirm adding camera (確認加入鏡頭). Pairing alone does not start video.
  3. Tap Start companionship (開始陪伴). On first use, check the accompaniment-consent notice and choose Agree and start (同意並開始). For these demos, the developer authorizes access to synthetic content. Allow the microphone permission when prompted: the app requires it for its push-to-talk function, including when viewing a silent demo.
  4. The picture should display CareMind review demo and Camera A. Use the fullscreen icon (全螢幕查看), pinch to zoom, drag after zooming, and Reset (重設). End viewing with 結束 or 結束陪伴.
  5. To add camera B, stop viewing and open Manage cameras (管理鏡頭) or Settings and help → Pairing management (配對管理). Repeat the paste/check/confirm steps with B. In My cameras (我的鏡頭), select A or B and press Start companionship. The app stores at most two cameras and views one at a time; switching ends the previous session. You may rename each selection with Save camera name (儲存鏡頭名稱) to distinguish A and B.

QR alternative: display this page on a second screen and tap Scan pairing QR (掃描配對 QR 碼) in the app. Scan the matching A or B image, confirm, and start. The full link and QR contain the same invitation. Copy/paste also works if the scanner or browser link handling is unavailable.

No login, password, account registration, or one-time code is needed for these two demos. Optional two-step verification is off for these fresh demo cameras. Audio is silent; push-to-talk audio is discarded without playback or recording. The demos have no torch or physical battery-temperature reading. Review those hardware features and real audio using your own camera device below.

One active viewer is allowed per camera. If a camera is busy, end the earlier session before starting it on another phone. Leaving the viewer app or locking the viewer phone stops video, listening, and talk; reopen it and press Start companionship to resume.

3. Review camera mode and real-device features

Use two Android devices you control, each with the submitted app: one camera device and one family-viewer device. Your own camera supplies its own live picture, microphone, and supported hardware. Use a test scene with the people present agreeing to share it. No developer household camera is involved.

  1. On the camera device choose This device is the camera (這台當鏡頭). Tap Create private pairing (建立私人配對) if no pairing exists. Keep the default service settings.
  2. Check the local consent notice and tap Start sharing camera and sound (開始分享鏡頭與聲音). Allow camera and microphone, and on Android 13 or later also notifications. The app requires all of these to start camera sharing. Keep its visible sharing notification available.
  3. Open the Pairing and security tab (配對與安全); choose Show pairing QR (顯示配對 QR 碼) or Copy pairing link (複製配對連結). On your viewer device choose family mode, scan or paste that invitation, confirm adding your camera, accept the ordinary consent notice, allow its microphone permission, and start companionship.
  4. On the viewer, toggle Listen to on-site sound (聽現場聲音). Hold Push to talk (按住說話); release to stop talking and return to listening. Each talk hold is limited to 30 seconds. Real two-way audio can be reviewed with this device pair; the public demos intentionally remain silent.
  5. On the camera screen, use Switch front/rear camera (切換前後鏡頭) where supported and inspect battery level, charging state, and temperature. On the viewer open More (更多) on the viewer to inspect camera information and Camera fill light (鏡頭補光燈). Torch control is enabled only when the connected camera reports compatible hardware; an activation lasts about one minute at most and disconnecting turns it off. A front camera or tablet may not support it. Temperature is the camera device's battery reading when available, not a measurement of every component. The app displays temperature warnings and stops sharing at its protection threshold.
  6. Tap Stop sharing (停止分享) on the camera screen or its notification. Confirm the viewer stops receiving your camera. Camera sharing can continue while its screen is off through the visible foreground service; it does not begin automatically at boot. Switching the camera's role or opening its security settings first stops sharing.

A viewer has two saved-camera slots. If you added both public demos and want to add your own camera, remove only a demo from your viewer's Pairing management using 從此手機移除鏡頭. This affects that viewer's selection only. You can add the same public demo again using its unchanged invitation.

Optional two-step verification and revocation

On your own camera device, open Security verification settings (安全驗證設定). Choose Enable two-step verification (開啟兩步驟驗證), scan the authenticator setup QR with your authenticator app, enter the current six-digit code in 輸入驗證器目前 6 位數代碼, and tap Verify and enable (驗證並啟用). Return to the camera (返回鏡頭端) and manually start sharing again. In versions with join-time security verification, use that authenticator's next code while completing the viewer's camera join: enter 驗證器 6 位數代碼 and tap 驗證並完成配對. This verification does not start video or audio; after it completes, tap Start accompaniment (開始陪伴) separately. A successfully verified viewer is remembered for that camera and normally does not need another code at each start. Earlier versions may instead prompt for the first code when starting accompaniment. Authenticator setup QR codes are separate from pairing QR codes and should stay on your own devices.

To test trust revocation, use 要求所有家屬重新驗證 on your own camera; pairing is retained but a new verification will be required when two-step verification is enabled. To test complete pairing revocation, use 撤銷配對並重設驗證器 on your own camera and confirm success, then create a new pairing for that test device. These actions apply to your own camera; the public demo cameras have no camera-side security controls on this page.

4. Other screens and connection help

  • Consent and pairing: Settings and help → Accompaniment consent (陪伴同意) lets you review or Withdraw consent (撤回同意); pairing is retained. Removing a camera from a viewer does not revoke other viewers. Full revocation is performed locally on that camera.
  • Help, privacy, and test ads: open Settings and help → Privacy information (隱私說明) or About CareMind (關於守心) for the full policy. Family standby may show Google test ads. Newer versions may also show a separate top banner after video connects and the first frame appears; it is hidden during connection, security verification, held talk, dialogs, full-screen viewing, or limited-height layouts. Camera-side banners, when supported, appear only while not sharing. If privacy choices are presented, make your own choice normally; Ad privacy options (廣告隱私選項) appear when required. Standard connection and SDK data handling is described in the policy; these demo images expose no household footage or pairing data.
  • If a demo does not connect: confirm the complete invitation was pasted, the microphone permission is allowed, and no other viewer is using that camera. End and retry the session. If your network blocks peer-to-peer WebRTC, try ordinary Wi-Fi that permits WebRTC. These demos have no TURN relay; connectivity on every restricted network is not guaranteed.

The app provides live, consent-based video and conversation. It does not record video or audio, provide AI analysis, send LINE/SMS monitoring alerts, or provide emergency response.
